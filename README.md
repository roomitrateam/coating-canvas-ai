## THE PROMPT

Build a professional service-business website for **Crown Detailing & Coating**, a mobile/studio auto detailing and ceramic coating company based in North Carolina. Use this exact business name everywhere — do not vary it (no "CrownCoatings," no "Crown Detail & Coatings").

**Business basics:**
- Phone: 919-607-2863
- Email: info@crowndetailstudio.com
- Service area: North Carolina — [insert specific city/county, e.g., "Raleigh, Cary, and the surrounding Triangle area"]
- Social: Instagram @crowndetailnc, Facebook
- Owner: Raj Rooprai, 5+ years of experience, certified System X ceramic coating installer

**Brand tone:** Confident, hands-on, craftsman-quality — not corporate. Every vehicle is hand-detailed, not run through an automated tunnel. Emphasize the owner-operated, personally-overseen nature of the work.

**Design direction:** Clean, premium automotive aesthetic. Dark charcoal/black base with a metallic accent color (silver or deep red). Landscape (16:9) hero imagery throughout — do not mix portrait and landscape hero orientations across pages. One consistent aspect ratio (4:5 portrait) for all service/package card images so grids look even. High-resolution automotive photography — no visible stock-photo watermarks or generic imagery.
